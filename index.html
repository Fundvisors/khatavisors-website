Questions shop owners ask

Is the Starter plan really free?

Yes. One user can make up to 100 bills a month at no cost. Move to Enterprise when you need a second user, bills on WhatsApp, photo-scanned purchases or e-invoices.

Which printer do I need?

Any Bluetooth thermal printer works with the KhataVisors Android app. On a Windows PC, the free print helper prints bills straight to your printer with no print window. A4 GST invoices print on any normal printer.

How are e-invoice and e-way bill charged?

On Enterprise they cost ₹0.50 per invoice, on top of the plan. You only pay for the ones you make.

Can I bring my items and customers from Excel?

Yes. Use "Import from Excel" on the Products and Customers pages to add them all at once, and enter old khata and supplier dues as opening balances.

Who can see my business data?

Only your business. Each business sees only its own bills and customers. Give every staff member their own login with the right role, and turn on two-step verification for extra safety.

My business is different. Will it fit?

KhataVisors has separate setups for retail, restaurants, cafés, pharmacies, wholesale, salons and amusement parks. If you need something more, the Custom plan adds your branding and paid customisation. Message us and we'll show you.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Start on Starter and upgrade whenever you need more. Talk to us to switch between monthly and yearly.
